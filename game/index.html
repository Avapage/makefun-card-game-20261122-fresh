    SIGILBOUND

    A duel of four sigils. One card each round. Read Vex's hand — and make him regret showing it.

    1 · Pick one card from your hand. It costs energy; energy grows every round.
    2 · Both cards are revealed at once. The sigil wheel, the Attuned sigil and raw power decide the round.
    3 · The loser takes the difference as damage. First duelist to 0 life falls.
    Keys · 1–9 select a card · Enter plays it · Esc puts it back · R starts a new duel.

    20 life each · 30-card decks · Vex plays face-up
